Questions people ask

How much do motorised curtains cost in Coimbatore?

The motor starts from ₹15,000 plus GST. The curtain fabric is priced like any other curtain, from ₹250 per metre plus GST. Enter your window size on this page to see both.

Can I open them from my phone?

Yes, we have fitted blinds that are controlled from a mobile app. There is a video of one on this page. Ask our team which motor suits your windows.

What can be motorised?

Curtains, blinds including outdoor patio blinds, skylight shades and Pergoflex roofs.

Can my existing curtains be motorised?

Ask our team. It depends on your track and window, and we check it when we visit.

Who fits the motor?

Our own team. Everything is checked at our factory before it leaves, and we inspect it again at your home after fitting.

Is the price on this page final?

No, it is a rough guide based on our starting rates, plus GST. The motor, fabric and fittings you choose decide the final figure, and we confirm it after measuring at your home.
